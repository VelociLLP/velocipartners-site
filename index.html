What We Do

Everything behind your numbers, handled

One team for the day-to-day bookkeeping and the big-picture advice — so nothing falls between your bookkeeper and your accountant.

Bookkeeping & Reconciliation

Transactions categorised, bank and card accounts reconciled, and a clean general ledger you can rely on every single month.

Financial Statements

Profit & loss, balance sheet and cash flow prepared to standard — with plain-English commentary on what actually moved.

Month-End Close

Accruals, prepayments, depreciation and journals posted on a fixed calendar, so your books close on time without the scramble.

Payroll & Compliance

Payroll processed, filings tracked and deadlines met — with a clear record behind every submission if anyone ever asks.

CFO & Growth Advisory

Budgets, forecasts, pricing and cash runway. Senior financial thinking on tap, without the cost of a full-time hire.

Systems & Automation

The right accounting stack, set up properly and connected — so data flows in cleanly instead of being retyped every month.
